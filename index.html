[ DIRECTIVES // PROTOCOLOS ]

Operational Directives

Every descent into the dark lunar facilities demands tight coordination, risk assessment, and resource preservation.

// DIRECTIVE 01

The Ark Quotas & Biomass

The Ark demands a constant supply of Biomass to sustain humanity. Meet the quota by bringing back captured enemies and biological artifacts, compressing both inside your ship's processing chambers into pure biomass.

// DIRECTIVE 02

Capture & Compression Protocol

Killing enemies yields little. Real value comes from capturing hostile creatures and hauling them to your ship, where the compression system processes them alongside biological artifacts into biomass for the quota.

// DIRECTIVE 03

Proximity Voice Audio

Immerse yourself with realistic 3D directional voice chat. Whisper when predators are near, hear your friends' screams fade down dark corridors, and use walkie-talkies to stay connected.

// DIRECTIVE 04

Procedural Facility Layouts

No two excursions are identical. Explore modular subterranean warehouses, bio-research facilities, and mansions with shifting routes, environmental traps, and evolving threat hazards.

// DIRECTIVE 05

Minibot Recon Protocol

Death doesn't bench you. Fallen operatives boot up into agile scout Minibots, providing real-time camera reconnaissance, spotting monster paths, and guiding teammates safely to the airlock.

// DIRECTIVE 06

Artifacts, Currency & Upgrades

Scavenged artifacts serve two purposes. Biological artifacts are compressed in the ship into biomass for the quota, while other artifacts grant money to upgrade equipment, sensors, and gadgets in the armory.
