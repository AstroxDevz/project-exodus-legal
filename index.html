PROJECT EXODUS

A tense cooperative extraction survival thriller. Infiltrate abandoned planetary complexes, extract volatile biomass, capture hostile alien specimens, and survive the quota deadline.

Core Gameplay Loop

Survive the Extraction

Every mission into the deep sector is an escalation of risk, teamwork, and resource management.

Biomass Harvest & Quotas

Contractual deadlines won't wait. Extract vital alien biomass and ancient relics from deep complexes to meet ever-increasing syndicate quotas before your time expires.

Specimen Capture

Dead enemies yield scrap, but live specimens pay fortune. Deploy containment traps, coordinate baiting tactics, and haul hostile entities back to the ship for massive rewards.

Procedural Facilities

No two drops are identical. Explore modular subterranean facilities with shifting room layouts, atmospheric hazards, security traps, and unpredictable spawn threats.

Minibot Protocol

Death is not the end of the round. Fallen squadmates transition into agile scout Minibots, guiding survivors through dark corridors, spotting threats, and enabling clutch extractions.

Cooperative Survival

Co-op Paced for High Tension

Team up with up to 4 players online. Upgrade your suits, scanner equipment, navigation sensors, and ship systems in the preparation phase before descending into hostile lunar sectors.
