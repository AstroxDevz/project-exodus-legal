Core Gameplay Loop

Survive the High-Stakes Extraction

Every descent into the dark lunar facilities demands tight coordination, risk assessment, and resource preservation.

Biomass Harvest & Syndicate Quotas

Earth was abandoned after a devastating war. Infiltrate mutant-infested complexes to harvest essential Biomass for The Ark while salvaging valuable artifacts to secure your own paycheck.

Live Specimen Containment

Eliminating enemies grants little, but capturing them alive pays a fortune. Coordinate specialized Net-Launchers, traps, and team flanking to bag dangerous specimens and bring them to extraction.

Proximity Voice Audio

Immerse yourself with realistic 3D directional voice chat. Whisper when predators are near, hear your friends' screams fade down dark corridors, and use walkie-talkies to stay connected.

Procedural Complex Generation

No two excursions are identical. Explore modular subterranean warehouses, bio-research facilities, and mansions with shifting routes, environmental traps, and evolving threat hazards.

Minibot Recon Protocol

Death doesn't bench you. Fallen operatives boot up into agile scout Minibots, providing real-time camera reconnaissance, spotting monster paths, and guiding teammates safely to the airlock.

Shared Arsenal & Equipment

Pool hard-earned credits to purchase specialized gadgets, high-voltage tasers, sonar radars, thermal vision sensors, and jump-thruster upgrades before your drop.

Ark Intelligence Database

Hostile Fauna & Entities

Study confirmed biological entities discovered across abandoned sector complexes. Learn their behavior to survive — and to capture them alive.

Built for High Tension & Unforgiving Survival
